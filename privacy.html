Privacy Policy

Last updated: 10 November 2026

This page describes the intended privacy practices for VikasKush, a personal lifestyle application currently under development. It must be reviewed and updated to match the app's actual implementation before the app is distributed to other users.

Information the app may handle

Depending on the features you use, Vikaskush may process account information, schedules and reminders, and document information such as document names, categories, descriptions, dates, and file references.

Cloud services

Vikaskush currently uses Firebase Authentication for app sign-in and Cloud Firestore for app data. A Google Drive integration is being developed for document file storage. If enabled, Google Drive will handle files according to the permissions you grant through Google's authorization flow. The app should request only the permissions needed for its features.

Do not upload sensitive documents to the Documents feature until the integration, access controls, encryption and recovery behaviour have been implemented and tested.

Encryption and device storage

Client-side encryption and offline document storage are planned features, not a statement that every current build already provides them. This policy must be revised to describe the final encryption design, key recovery, temporary files and offline-copy controls before those features are released.

Sharing and disclosure

Vikaskush does not intend to make private documents publicly accessible. Any future sharing feature should require an explicit user action. Data may be processed by the cloud providers required to operate features you choose to use, subject to their terms and privacy policies.

Retention and deletion

Users should be provided with a way to remove documents and associated metadata. The final app behaviour must specify whether deletion also removes cloud files, previous versions, cached copies and backups. Until that behaviour is implemented and documented, do not rely on this draft as a deletion guarantee.

Children

Vikaskush is not designed specifically for children. If the intended audience changes, this policy must be reviewed for applicable requirements.

Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated as the app develops. The latest version will be published on this page with an updated date.
